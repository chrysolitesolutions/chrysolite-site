    Questions

    Common questions

    Anything else, ask us in the form below.

    How short can an engagement be?

    A Surge Sprint runs 1 to 3 months. Scoping and team matching take the first week, calibration runs into week 2, a test batch runs in week 3 and full production starts in week 4. Longer programs run as a Project Pod of 3 months or more. For smaller QA audits we can scope a fixed-size review that finishes sooner.

    Do you work in our labeling tools?

    Yes. Our specialists work in your platform of choice, whether that's an in-house tool or a commercial annotation platform. If you don't have one, we can set up a workspace for the project.

    How do you keep quality high when scaling quickly?

    Every new specialist passes a calibration batch scored against your gold set before touching production data. Peer review, consensus and daily lead audits continue for the life of the project.

    Who owns the data and the labels?

    You do. All data, labels, guidelines and derived work belong to you. Specialists sign NDAs, and access is revoked when the project closes.

    Can you handle specialist domains?

    We match specialists by domain for work that needs it, such as finance, legal, healthcare, code and multilingual tasks. Tell us the domain when you request a team.
