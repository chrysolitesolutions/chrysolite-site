Location checker

Compare the location in THE FILE SAYS (SeekOut) with PUBLIC PROFILE SAYS (ZIPLabs or LinkedIn). Both places are looked up on the map, compared level by level (country, state, county or district, metro, city, neighbourhood) and given a % match, the distance and a suggested answer.

Rule: same county or district = Match. A town or suburb and its city in the same county = Match. “Greater X Area” or “X Metro” = city X. A missing state or country is fine. A different county or district = Not a match, even in the same state. A different country = Not a match.
