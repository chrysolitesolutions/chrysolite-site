Location checker

Compare the location in THE FILE SAYS (SeekOut) with PUBLIC PROFILE SAYS (ZIPLabs or LinkedIn). Both places are looked up on the map, compared level by level (country, state, metro area, county or district, city, neighbourhood) and given a % match, the distance and a suggested answer.

Rule: same metro area = Match. A suburb and its main city are the same. “Greater X Area” or “X Metro” = city X. A different metro area, even in the same state, is Not a match. A different country = Not a match.
