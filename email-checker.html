Email checker

Is this email address real, and does it belong to this person? The checker looks the email up in public sources that can prove it (GitHub, PGP key servers, Gravatar), checks the email's domain, compares the email with the person's name and LinkedIn, and gives you one-click searches for everything else. Match only with a page that shows the email with this person.

More data from LinkedIn (optional): paste the profile page text

Open the LinkedIn profile (and its Contact info), press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C, and paste here. The checker reads any emails, websites and usernames in it.
